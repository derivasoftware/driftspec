DriftSpec is built with the software below. The tool, the Python wheel and the engine compiled to
WebAssembly carry code from the first four; their licences require this notice to go with them.
GoogleTest builds the tests only and is not in anything DriftSpec ships.

| [CLI11](https://github.com/CLIUtils/CLI11) | 2.4.2 | BSD-3-Clause | ds |
| [miniz](https://github.com/richgel999/miniz) | 3.0.2 | MIT | ds, the wheel, ds.wasm |

The site of the playground loads these from public CDNs when it is opened; they are not part of
this repository or of what it ships: [CodeMirror](https://codemirror.net/5/) 5.65.16 (MIT),
[web-tree-sitter](https://github.com/tree-sitter/tree-sitter/tree/master/lib/binding_web) 0.26.13
(MIT), [marked](https://github.com/markedjs/marked) 12.0.2 (MIT), and the fonts Figtree and
JetBrains Mono from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1).

The grammar in `grammar/` is generated by the tree-sitter CLI; `grammar/src/tree_sitter/*.h` are
the headers the generator writes beside every parser, under tree-sitter's MIT licence.
